Brand guidelines · by CappyLabs

The idea

A cairn is a small stack of stones people build to mark a spot and guide the few who follow. It fits the app: quietly marking where you are, for a trusted few. The brand is calm, minimal and private — never loud or surveillant.

"Your location, for the few you trust."

The mark

Three stacked stones with a single lichen-green pebble on top. Use it on light, on dark, or inside the slate app icon. Keep clear space around it of at least the height of the top pebble. Minimum size 16 px.

Wordmark

Always lowercase cairn, set in Manrope SemiBold with tight tracking (about −0.03 em). Never capitalise, stretch, or add effects. Endorsed form: cairn by CappyLabs.

cairn

Palette

Five tones. Lichen is the only accent — use it sparingly for a single point of emphasis (the pebble, a live dot, a primary action). Everything else is the neutral stone family.

Slate#1F2A2E · text, marks, dark UI
Stone#6F7A7E · secondary text
Pebble#C9CFCD · borders, dividers
Mist#EDF0EE · backgrounds
Lichen#8FA35D · the only accent

Typography

Manrope throughout — SemiBold (600) for the wordmark and headings, Regular (400) for UI and body. Fallback stack: Manrope, system-ui, "Segoe UI", sans-serif.

Manrope SemiBold — headings & wordmark
Manrope Regular — the quiet, legible voice for everything else in the interface.

Do & don't

Do

  • Keep generous clear space around the mark.
  • Use Lichen for a single accent only.
  • Keep the wordmark lowercase.
  • Prefer calm, spacious layouts on Mist.

Don't

  • Recolour the pebble anything but Lichen.
  • Capitalise, outline, or add shadows to the wordmark.
  • Stretch, rotate, or crowd the mark.
  • Flood large areas with Lichen.
