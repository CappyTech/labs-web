Delete your Cairn data

Cairn is developed by CappyLabs. This page explains how to delete the data associated with your Cairn account.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Cairn.
  2. Tap the menu (⋮) → Backup & restore.
  3. Under Danger zone, tap Delete this identity and confirm.

This permanently and immediately deletes your account and all associated data from the server. It cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email privacy@cappylabs.uk with the subject "Cairn data deletion". If you have it, include your device's public key (shown in Backup & restore) so we can locate the account. We complete deletion within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your account / device identity (your public key and display name)
  • Your connections (the people you paired with)
  • Your shared locations — which are end-to-end encrypted and cannot be read by CappyLabs
  • Your pairing requests and presence timestamps (last-seen)

Deletion cascades: removing your account removes all of the above.

What is kept, and for how long

  • We do not retain personal data tied to your identity after deletion.
  • Your location content is end-to-end encrypted at all times — CappyLabs never has access to it.
  • Routine server security logs (which do not contain your location content) may persist for up to 30 days, then are purged.
  • If you self-host your own Cairn server, your data resides only on your server and is removed when you delete your account there.
